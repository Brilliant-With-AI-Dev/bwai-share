Watch Tev’s note

  • Private until you say otherwise.

    Nothing reaches Tev until you’ve read your answers and approved them. You can leave any answer out.

  • Rough is the point.

    Tev wants what you actually think, not a polished version. Say it the way you’d say it to him.

  • Leave and come back.

    Progress is saved after every answer. Close the tab and pick up where you were.

How this works

  • Your conversation stays private.

    Nobody sees what you say here. Only answers you approve leave it.

  • You read your answers before anyone else.

    After the last part, Mirror lays out your answers in your words. Fix anything that’s wrong; you can also leave any answer out.

  • Approved answers go to Tev with your name.

    Tev (Organizational Development) reads them to prepare the planning sessions. Nothing changes after you approve.

  • Dictation is transcribed live.

    If you speak your answers, the audio is turned into text as you go. We keep the words, not the recording.

Mirror works best out loud
