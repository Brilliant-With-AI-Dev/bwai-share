What remains before a usable pilot

The prototype proves flow and contracts only.

Identity and privacy

  • Authenticated organization membership and role assignment.
  • Server-enforced visibility for every context asset.
  • Auditable consent, withdrawal, release, and deletion.
  • Retention rules and production privacy review.

Core context assets

  • Organization, user, and task-scoped persistence.
  • Real versions, snapshots, provenance, and lifecycle rules.
  • Concurrent access and conflict handling.
  • Reliable restart, recovery, and data export.

AI and prompts

  • Real interview, distillation, structuring, and synthesis calls.
  • BWAI-authored prompt ingestion and version approval.
  • Evaluation across organizations and leadership roles.
  • Evidence citations, redaction, and failure handling.

Team operations

  • Invitations, deadlines, progress, and reminders.
  • Secure BWAI review and toolkit handoff.
  • Participant acknowledgement before the meeting.
  • Support for incomplete or withdrawn participation.

Planning meeting

  • AI-supported collaborative discussion remains undesigned.
  • Leadership decisions require explicit capture.
  • Final priorities, owners, milestones, and action plans.
  • Post-meeting updates and ongoing accountability.

Pilot evidence

  • Leadership candor and trust need direct testing.
  • BWAI synthesis workload remains unknown.
  • Consent comprehension needs user testing.
  • Planning quality requires agreed success measures.
