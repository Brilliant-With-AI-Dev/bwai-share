How it goes

Three steps, in this order.

01

You talk.

Not a form and not a quiz. The conversation follows what you say, digs where it matters, drops what does not. Nothing is scored.

02

It writes to you.

A letter, in prose, about how you work — the pattern you are stuck in, the thing you keep circling, the conversation you have been avoiding.

03

You get a map.

Every piece of the job with a verdict on it. Each one arrives with the thing you need to act on it: the prompt pack, the handover brief, the case to take upstairs.

Before you ask.

Is this an assessment?

No. Nothing is scored or ranked, and there is no profile type at the end.

Do I need to know anything about AI?

No. It asks what you already know how to answer — how your week goes, what you own, what keeps slipping.

How long, honestly?

About forty-five minutes, across roughly twenty-five questions. Team Mirror is shorter per person.

What does my team see?

The group picture — where you agree and where you do not. How individual answers are handled is agreed with your team up front.
